# Cedarpad positioning

_Aligned 2026-07-18 (James + Claude session). This is the canonical product
thesis. Marketing copy, page information architecture, and roadmap ranking start
from here; changing it is a deliberate decision, not drift._

## Thesis

**Alignment — between people, and between people and AI — requires a shared
medium richer than text. Cedarpad is that medium.**

## The two drivers

1. **Teams: planning is still hard.** Vibe coding made building nearly free, so
   the bottleneck moved upstream. A team of engineers all vibe coding don't get
   aligned — they get lost, fast, in different directions. AI made misalignment
   cheap to produce at scale. Cedarpad is the whiteboard where teams use AI to
   get aligned. The crux is planning, brainstorming, sharing — collaboration —
   not execution.

2. **Chat isn't visual.** So much of what an AI needs to communicate is
   structural; prose serializes it lossily. Cedarpad is the standard surface
   where an AI can *show* instead of describe — and the human can answer in
   kind (point, drag, annotate).

## The soul: live components

The unit of communication on the pad is a **rendered, working thing** — mermaid,
markdown, charts, custom React components, whole interface variants (landing
pages, nav shells). Building new ones or reusing ones that exist. Freehand
shapes are annotation glue (arrows, circles), not the medium itself.

- **Killer ritual — variants side-by-side:** when a decision is fuzzy, the AI
  renders the option space as N live artifacts and the team aligns by pointing
  at real things instead of arguing about descriptions.
- **The component library is accumulated vocabulary:** every component built
  becomes a word the team and its agents share. Conversations get faster over
  time — a compounding advantage a fresh chat window structurally can't have.

## Billing order

1. **The surface** — visual communication between humans and AI.
2. **The team** — convergence: live presence, one shared inbox, decisions that
   bind everyone.
3. **Execution** — components and PRs as *proof the plan was real*. Third
   billing, never the headline. "Delegation you can watch" / "AI does real
   work for you" is explicitly NOT the lead (an earlier framing, corrected).

## Problem language

> AI made building easy. Aligning your team is still hard.

Visitors are problem-aware, not product-aware; lead with the wound, briefly,
then show the medium.

## Product implication

The center of gravity is the **component experience** — the palette, placed
instances, fullscreen, and (future) first-class side-by-side variant
comparison — over drawing features. tldraw is the spatial substrate, not the
product.
