# YC Requests for Startups (Current: Summer 2026)

## How RFS alignment is used in applications

RFS alignment is a **signal, not a requirement**. YC states explicitly that most companies it funds do not fit any RFS, and it funds ideas across the board. An RFS tells applicants which problem spaces individual YC partners currently believe are newly solvable and under-attacked; matching one can strengthen an application's "why now" framing and show market awareness, but shoehorning a company into an RFS category (or pivoting to chase one) reads as weak. Critics should treat an RFS match as a modest positive when genuine, a neutral when absent, and a negative when the fit looks forced.

Context for Summer 2026: the batch runs July through September in San Francisco; YC invests $500,000 in every accepted company on standard terms. The list's framing: "AI has stopped being a feature and started being the foundation," with a visible tilt toward hard tech (agriculture robotics, counter-drone defense, space, semiconductors) alongside AI-native software plays.
